Hermes has a memory now. I added Honcho for that.

Honcho is a memory layer for agents. Conversations go into it as messages, grouped into sessions, and every message belongs to a peer. I'm one peer and the agent is another. Honcho keeps the messages, and in the background it works through them and builds up a picture of each peer: what they've said, what they seem to prefer, what keeps coming back.

Hermes can ask Honcho for that. It can pull up what's relevant from earlier sessions, or put a plain question about me and get back whatever Honcho has worked out so far. The model underneath still forgets everything the moment a reply is done. Honcho is the part that doesn't.

So the agent and its memory are two separate things. Hermes does the talking. Honcho holds on to what was said and keeps thinking about it after I've closed the chat.

It'll also remember the things I'd rather it forgot. I'll find out which ones those are.
